## Workspace Settings — Teams

This section explains how to create, manage, and join workspace teams.

_For a list of all guides on the TCAT Wiki, refer to the [Guides List](../../../guides-list/index.md)._{ .guides-list-ref }

---

### Open Teams

1. **Open** a workspace from the [Dashboard](../dashboard.md)
2. **Open** **Workspace Settings**
3. **Select** **Teams**

The **Teams** page lists each team's name and member count. Each team row can include controls to open team settings, view the team's invitation QR code, and delete the team. Use **New Team** to create a team when team management is available for your account.

![Workspace Settings Teams page showing the New Team button, team names, member counts, team settings, QR code, and delete controls](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/01-teams-light.avif#only-light)
![Workspace Settings Teams page showing the New Team button, team names, member counts, team settings, QR code, and delete controls](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/01-teams-dark.avif#only-dark)

### Create a team

1. **Select** **New Team**
2. Enter the team name in **Team Name**
3. **Select** **Create**

![Create a Team dialog with the Team Name field and Create button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/02-create-team-light.avif#only-light)
![Create a Team dialog with the Team Name field and Create button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/02-create-team-dark.avif#only-dark)

The new team appears in the list on the **Teams** page.

---

### Rename a team

1. Find the team you want to rename
2. **Select** **Open Team Settings**
3. Replace the value in **Team Name**
4. **Select** **Update**

![Team Settings dialog with the Team Name field and Update button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/03-team-settings-light.avif#only-light)
![Team Settings dialog with the Team Name field and Update button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/03-team-settings-dark.avif#only-dark)

The updated team name appears in the team list.

### Review team members

1. Find the team you want to inspect
2. **Select** the member-count control
3. Review the **Team Members** dialog
4. **Select** **Close** when you are finished

![Team Members dialog listing members and member removal controls](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/04-team-members-light.avif#only-light)
![Team Members dialog listing members and member removal controls](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/04-team-members-dark.avif#only-dark)

The **Team Members** dialog lists the people currently associated with the team. Use the remove control next to a member when you need to remove that member from the team.

---

### Share a team invitation

1. Find the team you want to share
2. **Select** **View QR Code**
3. In the **Join this Team** dialog, share the displayed QR code or shareable link
4. **Select** **Download QR Code** when you need a saved copy of the QR code
5. **Select** **Close** when you are finished

![Join this Team dialog showing the team QR code, shareable link, Download QR Code button, and Close button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/05-join-this-team-light.avif#only-light)
![Join this Team dialog showing the team QR code, shareable link, Download QR Code button, and Close button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/05-join-this-team-dark.avif#only-dark)

People who open the invitation can review the team and workspace before joining. Teams associate subsequent edits with the selected team for attribution.

!!! note "Teams and permissions"

    Teams are workspace-scoped attribution labels for edits. They do not grant or revoke workspace permissions and are separate from TDEI project groups.

---

### Join a team

1. Open the shared invitation link or scan the team's QR code
2. Review the **Join Team** page, including the invited team and workspace
3. **Select** **Join Team**

![Join Team page showing the invitation, workspace, current team members, and Join Team button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/06-join-team-light.avif#only-light)
![Join Team page showing the invitation, workspace, current team members, and Join Team button](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/06-join-team-dark.avif#only-dark)

The **Join Team** page identifies the team and workspace and shows the current **On the team** list. Joining associates your subsequent edits with that team for attribution; it does not change your workspace role.

---

### Delete a team

1. Find the team you want to delete
2. **Select** **Delete**
3. Check the team name in the **Delete Team** confirmation dialog
4. **Select** **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the team

![Delete Team confirmation dialog naming the team and showing Cancel and Delete buttons](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/07-delete-team-light.avif#only-light)
![Delete Team confirmation dialog naming the team and showing Cancel and Delete buttons](../../../resources/images/workspaces/user-manual/05-settings/08-teams/07-delete-team-dark.avif#only-dark)

Confirm the deletion only after checking that the dialog names the intended team. If a required team-management control is unavailable, contact a workspace administrator.
