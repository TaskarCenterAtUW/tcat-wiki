# What statistics does Walksheds provide?

## Short Answer

Walkshed Information can summarize reachable footways, sidewalks, crossings, curb conditions, paved edges, and total reachable length. Extension datasets may add point and polygon information.

## Significance

Statistics provide a compact way to compare modeled network results and identify questions for further review.

## What This Means

The panel may show counts and percentages for marked, unmarked, ramped, unramped, paved, and unpaved edges, plus total walkshed length. Sidewalk Score is documented as experimental and may not be enabled.

## What This Does Not Mean

Counts and percentages depend on the dataset, budget, profile, and reachable graph. They do not certify condition, safety, or legal compliance.

## How To Use This

Record the scenario settings and dataset version with statistics. Compare like-for-like results and inspect the map.

## Example

A planner compares the percentage of ramped crossings under two scenarios while holding the dataset and origin constant.

## Assistant Guidance

Name the statistic and scenario context. Avoid presenting Sidewalk Score as settled or universal.
