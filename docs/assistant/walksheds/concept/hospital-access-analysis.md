# How can walksheds support hospital access analysis?

## Short Answer

Walksheds can support hospital-access analysis by showing modeled pedestrian reachability to hospital entrances, clinics, transit connections, or related destinations under selected profiles and travel limits.

## Significance

Patients, visitors, staff, and people accompanying others may use different approaches and have different accessibility needs. Network analysis can reveal barriers that a facility-radius map misses.

## What This Means

Define the facility entrance, origins, destinations, profiles, travel limits, network, and release. Include current campus or hospital information, construction, private access, transit, field evidence, and community knowledge.

## What This Does Not Mean

A hospital compares wheelchair and pedestrian walksheds to its public entrance and nearby transit stops, then verifies crossings, doors, construction, and internal wayfinding locally.

## How To Use This

A hospital walkshed does not guarantee access to a building, medical service, emergency care, or an accessible transfer. It does not replace facility assessment or clinical and emergency guidance.

## Example

Use the result for access screening and planning, not urgent navigation. Document entrances and assumptions, validate high-consequence routes, and consult current facility information.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask which facility, entrance, profile, destination, and date are involved. Cite the data and local sources, avoid health or compliance claims, and abstain when access restrictions are unknown.

## Related Concepts

Hospital access analysis must distinguish public pedestrian reachability from entry, service, and operational access.
