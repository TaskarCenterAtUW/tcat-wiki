# How can walksheds support economic development?

## Short Answer

Walksheds can support economic-development analysis by showing modeled access to jobs, services, businesses, and other destinations under selected pedestrian profiles and travel limits.

## Significance

The results can help planners identify areas where pedestrian connections may support access to destinations or where network gaps warrant further investigation.

## What This Means

Define the destinations, geography, profiles, cost limits, data version, and economic question. Combine walkshed results with land use, transit, demographic, market, safety, and community information.

## What This Does Not Mean

A planner compares modeled access to downtown services from nearby housing areas and uses the result to identify possible pedestrian improvements for further study.

## How To Use This

A walkshed does not predict business activity, prove that a network causes economic outcomes, or show equal access for every person. It does not replace economic or community analysis.

## Example

Use walksheds to frame a bounded access question, document assumptions, validate important findings, and avoid causal claims unsupported by study design.

## Assistant Guidance

Name the destinations, profile, network, and release. Cite the analysis, distinguish reachability from economic impact, and abstain when the intended economic outcome is not defined.
