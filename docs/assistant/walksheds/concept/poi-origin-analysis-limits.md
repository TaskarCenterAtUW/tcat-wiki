# What are the limits of POI-origin walkshed analysis?

## Short Answer

POI-origin walksheds emphasize areas reachable from the destinations represented in the POI inventory. They do not provide equivalent walkshed metrics for every sidewalk in a jurisdiction.

## Significance

The result is destination-focused rather than a complete network-condition assessment.

## What This Means

Interpret Section 4 with the POI set, profiles, and travel budget used.

## What This Does Not Mean

Sidewalks outside those walksheds are not necessarily unimportant or inaccessible.

## How To Use This

Review the full network and local destinations alongside POI-origin metrics.

## Example

A sidewalk far from mapped POIs appears on the full network but is outside the calculated walkshed areas.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask which POIs and origins were used before interpreting coverage.
