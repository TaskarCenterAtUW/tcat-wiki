# What is the difference between a pedestrian walkshed and a wheelchair-user walkshed?

## Short Answer

A pedestrian walkshed and a wheelchair-user walkshed use different travel assumptions. The wheelchair-oriented result can apply additional constraints such as maximum uphill and downhill slopes and avoiding raised curbs and stairs.

## Significance

Comparing profiles reveals how infrastructure conditions affect modeled reachability for different travelers.

## What This Means

Use the profiles provided by the Walksheds tool and compare the results when using different profiles from the same dataset, origin, and cost limit.

## What This Does Not Mean

Neither profile represents every person's abilities, preferences, or current field conditions.

## How To Use This

Use the difference between pedestrian and wheelchair-user walksheds

## Example

A wheelchair-oriented walkshed is smaller near a steep or disconnected crossing while a general pedestrian profile reaches farther.

## Assistant Guidance

Avoid presenting profiles as medical or legal classifications.
