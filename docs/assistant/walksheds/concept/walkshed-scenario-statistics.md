# What statistics can Walksheds scenarios compare?

## Short Answer

Saved scenarios can expose computed coverage or total reachable network length for different profiles, budgets, times, or conditions. Batch results may not have one combined download.

## Significance

Statistics make modeled differences easier to summarize alongside map overlays.

## What This Means

Compare equivalent scenario outputs and document their assumptions.

## What This Does Not Mean

A larger reachable length is not automatically a better outcome for every traveler.

## How To Use This

Pair statistics with maps, profile definitions, local goals, and field evidence.

## Example

A permissive profile reaches more network length than a restricted wheelchair profile from the same origin.

## Assistant Guidance

Explain what changed and avoid ranking scenarios without context.
