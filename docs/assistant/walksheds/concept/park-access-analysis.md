# How can walksheds support park access analysis?

## Short Answer

Walksheds can support park-access analysis by showing modeled reachability to park entrances or destinations under selected pedestrian profiles, travel limits, and network assumptions.

## Significance

Network analysis can identify where crossings, barriers, missing connections, or terrain may affect modeled access to parks.

## What This Means

Define the park entrance rather than only its boundary, origins, destinations, profiles, travel limits, and release. Include current hours, gates, paths, terrain, transit, local conditions, and community experience where relevant.

## What This Does Not Mean

A planner compares wheelchair-profile access to several park entrances and then checks gates, path conditions, crossings, and construction information with the park authority.

## How To Use This

A parks walkshed does not prove that the park or entrance is open, accessible, safe, or suitable for every visitor. It does not replace park-management or field information.

## Example

Use results for screening and planning, label uncertain or private paths, validate important routes locally, and communicate assumptions clearly.

## Assistant Guidance

Name the entrance, profile, cost, network, and date. Avoid claims about visitation or universal access without supporting evidence.
