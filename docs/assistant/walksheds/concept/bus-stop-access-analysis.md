# How can Walksheds help analyze access to bus stops?

## Short Answer

Walksheds can show which areas are reachable from a bus stop under a selected travel profile and cost limit.

## Significance

This helps planners compare access to stops and identify gaps in the pedestrian network.

## What This Means

Choose the stop, dataset, profile, and maximum travel cost; compare results across stops or scenarios and inspect disconnected areas.

## What This Does Not Mean

Reachability is modeled, not a guarantee that a person can travel safely or comfortably in current conditions.

## How To Use This

Document profile, cost, dataset version, and limitations when reporting results.

## Example

A planner compares wheelchair-oriented walksheds around two stops and identifies a crossing gap for field review.

## Assistant Guidance

Explain the result as modeled access and cite the dataset and profile used.
