# What future capabilities are planned for Walksheds?

## Short Answer

Future Walksheds capabilities should be described only from a current, verified roadmap or product announcement. Do not treat proposed analyses, integrations, profile changes, or interface features as available until documented as current.

## Significance

Separating roadmap possibilities from current functionality prevents users from planning around features that may change, be deferred, or never be released.

## What This Means

When a roadmap is available, record its source and date and distinguish planned, experimental, and released capabilities. Direct users to the current product documentation for what they can use now.

## What This Does Not Mean

Mentioning a possible future capability is not a promise, schedule, commitment, or evidence that the feature will be delivered.

## How To Use This

Answer current-use questions from current documentation. Label future-facing information clearly, avoid unsupported feature lists, and provide a verified support or Office Hours path for clarification when appropriate.

## Example

A partner asks whether a future profile or data integration is planned. Staff explain that only a current roadmap can confirm this and describe the currently documented workflow separately.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent planned features or dates. Cite the current roadmap or state that no verified roadmap information is available, and abstain when the question requires internal confirmation.
