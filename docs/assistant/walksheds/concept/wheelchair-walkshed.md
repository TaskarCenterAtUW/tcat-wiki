# What does a wheelchair walkshed represent?

## Short Answer

A wheelchair walkshed represents the area reachable under a selected wheelchair-oriented profile, travel limit, network, barrier, crossing, elevation, and other cost assumptions.

## Significance

It can show how the modeled network changes when travel considerations relevant to wheelchair users are applied.

## What This Means

Record the profile, settings, origin, cost limit, dataset, release, and network assumptions. Interpret the result with lived experience and current local evidence.

## What This Does Not Mean

A wheelchair walkshed may exclude a steep or disconnected route that a general pedestrian analysis includes, producing a different reachable area.

## How To Use This

It does not represent every wheelchair user's needs, measure a person's ability, guarantee an accessible route, or certify compliance.

## Example

Use the result as a modeling aid, compare settings carefully, validate important routes locally, and explain missing or uncertain data.

## Assistant Guidance

Name the profile and data version, avoid universal claims about wheelchair users, and abstain when profile behavior or network conditions are unknown.
