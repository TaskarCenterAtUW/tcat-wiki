# How do I save and compare Walksheds scenarios?

## Short Answer

After generating a walkshed, choose **Save Scenario**, give it a name, and use the Edits tab to show, review, or delete saved scenarios.

## Significance

Named snapshots make profile, dataset, departure-time, and hypothetical-edit comparisons easier to reproduce.

## What This Means

A scenario stores the origin, budget, preferences, and resulting coverage. Selecting a scenario checkbox makes it visible on the map.

## What This Does Not Mean

A scenario is not a source-data release or proof that the modeled change will occur in the physical environment.

## How To Use This

Change one meaningful input at a time when possible and record dataset version and assumptions.

## Example

A planner saves baseline and raised-curb-removal scenarios and overlays them to inspect the difference in reachable paths.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask which inputs changed between scenarios before interpreting a difference.
