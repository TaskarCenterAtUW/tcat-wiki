# What role does field validation play?

## Short Answer

Field validation checks whether mapped pedestrian features and route assumptions correspond to current conditions. It is especially important when a route or planning decision depends on accessibility details, temporary conditions, or known data gaps.

## Significance

Field observations connect modeled results with lived conditions and can identify corrections or missing attributes.

## What This Means

- Select representative and high-consequence locations.
- Compare the route, geometry, attributes, and barriers with observations.
- Record date, observer context, unresolved issues, and proposed corrections.

## What This Does Not Mean

Field validation is not automatically a complete survey or a legal determination. A sample cannot prove that every feature or route is accurate.

## How To Use This

Use a documented sampling and review process, preserve evidence, and update conclusions when the data or field conditions change.

## Example

A municipality checks curb ramps, crossings, and surfaces along routes selected for a public project, then records discrepancies before using the analysis.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not overgeneralize from one observation. Cite the dataset and validation date, ask what was checked, and abstain from broad accuracy claims without representative evidence.
