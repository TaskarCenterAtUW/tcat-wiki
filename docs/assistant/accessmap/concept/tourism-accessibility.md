# Can AccessMap support tourism accessibility?

## Short Answer

AccessMap can support tourism accessibility by helping visitors and destination managers compare pedestrian routes under accessibility preferences. Results should be paired with current venue, surface, entrance, and service information.

## Significance

Accessible tourism depends on the route as well as the destination and its operations. A route model can improve trip planning while leaving important venue details to the destination owner.

## What This Means

- Identify destinations, entrances, profiles, and travel constraints.
- Review the route and source date, then check venue-specific information.
- Share limitations and provide a way to report observed changes.

## What This Does Not Mean

An AccessMap route does not certify a venue, guarantee current conditions, or represent every indoor or private path. A tourism use is not an accessibility or legal determination.

## How To Use This

Use route results as planning context and verify destination details with the venue or local authority.

## Example

A visitor compares routes to a museum under an accessibility preference, then confirms the accessible entrance and current elevator status with the museum.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not promise destination accessibility. Cite route assumptions and venue sources, ask for the destination and profile, and abstain when current venue information is missing.
