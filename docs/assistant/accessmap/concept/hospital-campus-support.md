# Can AccessMap support hospitals or medical campuses?

## Short Answer

AccessMap can support hospitals and medical campuses by helping compare pedestrian routes to entrances, transit stops, and related destinations under accessibility preferences. Campus-specific, indoor, private, and temporary information may require local sources.

## Significance

Patients, visitors, and staff may have time-sensitive access needs. Modeled routes can help identify questions, but hospital operations and current conditions remain essential.

## What This Means

- Define the destination, entrance, profile, and data version.
- Add current facility and access information where available.
- Verify construction, closures, and entrance conditions with the facility.

## What This Does Not Mean

An AccessMap route is not a medical-access guarantee, facility audit, emergency instruction, or ADA determination. It may omit indoor, restricted, or temporary paths.

## How To Use This

Use the result as a planning aid and confirm access details with the hospital or campus team before relying on it.

## Example

A hospital planner compares routes to two public entrances, then checks current construction and accessible-entrance information with facilities staff.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not promise access to care or a facility. Cite route and facility sources, ask for the entrance and profile, and direct urgent needs to the facility or emergency services.
