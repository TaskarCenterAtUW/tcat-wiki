# Can AccessMap support campus accessibility?

## Short Answer

AccessMap can support campus-accessibility planning by comparing pedestrian routes to buildings, transit stops, and other destinations under selected accessibility preferences. Campus-specific data and local verification may be needed for paths or conditions not represented in the general network.

## Significance

Campuses often include paths, entrances, and temporary conditions that differ from the surrounding street network. A route analysis can help identify questions for campus facilities and accessibility teams.

## What This Means

- Define the campus boundary, destinations, profiles, and dataset version.
- Add or consult appropriate local information where supported.
- Verify routes with campus staff and users before relying on them.

## What This Does Not Mean

AccessMap does not certify campus accessibility or guarantee that indoor, private, restricted, or temporary paths are represented. A route is not a substitute for an accessibility audit.

## How To Use This

Use AccessMap for comparison and gap identification, then pair results with campus records, field checks, and lived experience.

## Example

A university compares routes between accessible parking, transit stops, and classroom buildings, then verifies entrances and temporary barriers with campus staff.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for campus-specific data and the intended decision. Cite the profile and dataset, avoid completeness or compliance claims, and abstain when private or indoor access is central.
