# What is the difference between route planning and navigation in AccessMap?

## Short Answer

Route planning previews a trip between selected locations. Navigation or route following uses the device location and directions while the traveler is moving.

## Significance

The distinction helps users choose the correct workflow and helps documentation answer practical search questions. A planned route can be reviewed without beginning travel.

## What This Means

Use planning to compare profiles, routes, distance, estimated time, elevation, and steepest grades. Use route following when location-aware, step-by-step guidance is needed. Current-location features require browser and device location permission.

## What This Does Not Mean

Planning does not automatically track the traveler, and navigation does not remove the need to review profile settings. Both depend on available data and device support. The Trip Information panel states that routes are for informational purposes and that users should use their own judgment and follow local laws.

## How To Use This

Choose the mode based on the immediate goal. Enable location and relevant alerts before beginning route following.

## Example

A traveler previews three wheelchair profiles at home, then starts route following from their current location when they leave.

## Assistant Guidance

Use the user's goal rather than assuming they know the product terminology. Link to the appropriate planning or following workflow.
