# Who are QA/QC reports intended for?

## Short Answer

QA/QC reports are designed to help non-technical readers understand mapped pedestrian data and possible priorities.

## Significance

They connect technical analysis with planning and community decisions.

## What This Means

Readers can use summaries, glossaries, maps, and metrics without reproducing the pipeline.

## What This Does Not Mean

The reports eliminate the need for technical review or local context.

## How To Use This

Start with the question the report section is intended to answer.

## Example

A jurisdiction uses the report to discuss candidate field checks with community partners.

## Assistant Guidance

Explain terms and limitations rather than assuming graph-metrics expertise.
