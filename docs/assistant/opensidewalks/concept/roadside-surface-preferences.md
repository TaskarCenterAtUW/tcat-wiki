# How can roadside surface data support route preferences?

## Short Answer

Surface data such as paved, gravel, grass, or other unpaved materials can help travelers choose routes that fit their needs and conditions.

## Significance

Different people tolerate surfaces differently, especially with mobility devices or seasonal weather.

## What This Means

More granular schema fields can support configurable routing preferences.

## What This Does Not Mean

A surface label does not guarantee current traction, firmness, or accessibility.

## How To Use This

Treat surface information as decision support and consider weather and local knowledge.

## Example

A traveler accepts gravel shoulders but excludes grass during wet weather.

## Assistant Guidance

Avoid treating preferences as universal accessibility judgments.
