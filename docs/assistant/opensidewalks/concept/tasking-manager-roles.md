# What roles exist in the OSM US Tasking Manager?

## Short Answer

The OSM US Tasking Manager organizes users, projects, teams, campaigns, and organizations. OpenSidewalks uses separate mapper, validator, and project-manager teams with different participation paths.

## Significance

Roles and team membership determine which projects a contributor can access and whether they can validate other people's work.

## What This Means

The OpenSidewalks Mappers team is Team `27` and is open to anyone. The OpenSidewalks Validators team is Team `26` and can be joined by request by experienced mappers. The OpenSidewalks Project Managers team is Team `25` and is invite-only. OpenSidewalks operates within Organization `8`.

## What This Does Not Mean

Joining a mapper team does not automatically grant validator or project-manager permissions.

## How To Use This

Choose the team that matches the intended role, follow the current membership link, and confirm project-specific permissions before starting.

## Example

A new contributor joins Team `27` to map tasks, while an experienced mapper requests membership in Team `26` to validate work.

## Assistant Guidance

Treat the team IDs as stable identifiers but still verify current access and project permissions. Do not infer permissions solely from a user's OSM account.
