# Can this data support curb ramp inventories?

## Short Answer

OS-CONNECT can support a curb-ramp inventory as a screening or reference input when its coverage, attributes, version, and limitations fit the intended use. A jurisdiction should supplement it with authoritative local records and current field or inspection evidence.

## Significance

An inventory needs more than locations. Condition, dimensions, connections, ownership, inspection history, and other requirements may be outside the available dataset.

## What This Means

Use the dataset to identify candidate ramps and gaps, compare attributes, prioritize field review, and organize questions for local staff. Record source, date, confidence, missing fields, and review status.

## What This Does Not Mean

OS-CONNECT is not automatically a complete or authoritative curb-ramp inventory, and it does not certify compliance or replace an agency's inspection and asset-management process.

## How To Use This

Define the inventory's purpose and acceptance criteria, join the relevant local records, validate priority locations, and keep unverified or reference features clearly labeled.

## Example

An agency uses OS-CONNECT to find possible curb ramps, compares those records with its inspection database, and schedules field review for locations with missing or conflicting information.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for the jurisdiction, release, attributes, and intended decision. Cite the data and local inventory, avoid completeness or compliance claims, and abstain when the source coverage is unknown.
