# What does "participatory mapping" mean?

## Short Answer

Participatory mapping means involving people with local knowledge or lived experience in collecting, reviewing, interpreting, or prioritizing geographic information.

## Significance

It broadens the evidence available for pedestrian and accessibility-data work and can expose gaps that centralized or automated collection misses.

## What This Means

Set a clear purpose, provide accessible tools and instructions, record location and context, protect privacy, and explain how reports or edits will be reviewed.

## What This Does Not Mean

Participation does not make every contribution accurate, representative, authoritative, or automatically published. One participant does not speak for an entire community.

## How To Use This

Distinguish observations, corrections, and policy input; validate consequential findings; and communicate decisions and unresolved issues.

## Example

A community documents a blocked sidewalk, compares it with the current map, and submits the observation through the appropriate review channel.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not promise acceptance or consensus. Cite the participation process and data version, and abstain when the engagement scope is unclear.
