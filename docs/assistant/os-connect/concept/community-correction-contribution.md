# How can community members contribute corrections?

## Short Answer

Community members can contribute corrections by reporting a specific observed problem through the current documented channel. Useful reports identify the location, feature, dataset or release, observed and expected condition, date, and supporting evidence.

## Significance

Specific reports help reviewers distinguish a data problem from a change in the physical world and make follow-up more traceable.

## What This Means

Describe the observation without guessing, include the source version and relevant identifier or coordinates, protect personal information, and state whether the report concerns OpenStreetMap, OS-CONNECT, TDEI, or a downstream product.

## What This Does Not Mean

Submitting a correction does not automatically change a dataset, guarantee acceptance, or establish that the reported condition is true for every traveler.

## How To Use This

Use the verified reporting route, keep a copy of the report, respond to clarification requests, and check a later release or confirmed status rather than assuming that submission changed the data.

## Example

A resident reports that a mapped crossing is blocked, supplies the location and date, and identifies the release viewed. Reviewers can then determine whether the issue is temporary, local, or a dataset correction.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not promise a correction or timeline. Cite the current reporting guidance, distinguish observations from verified changes, and direct unresolved operational questions to the verified support email.
