# What are the community engagement pathways?

## Short Answer

Community engagement may include documented issue reporting, mapping activities, workshops or Office Hours, public meetings, local validation, surveys, and planning discussions. The available pathway depends on the question, community, product, and current program information.

## Significance

Different pathways support different kinds of contribution. Clear expectations help participants understand whether they are reporting an observation, learning a tool, reviewing priorities, or contributing to a formal decision.

## What This Means

Before participating, identify the goal, location, data or product, evidence, accessibility needs, privacy considerations, and desired follow-up. Use the current published channel for the relevant activity.

## What This Does Not Mean

Participation does not guarantee that every comment changes a dataset or decision, and one pathway is not appropriate for every issue. Engagement is not a substitute for legal, engineering, or emergency services.

## How To Use This

Explain the scope, decision owner, review process, timeline only when verified, and how feedback will be recorded or used. Offer accessible formats and avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information.

## Example

A group brings a set of mapped crossing concerns to Office Hours, separates urgent hazards from data questions, and uses the documented issue channel for locations that need a formal review.

## Assistant Guidance

Name only current, verified pathways. Distinguish Office Hours, support, issue reporting, and agency engagement, and abstain when current event or contact details are unknown.
