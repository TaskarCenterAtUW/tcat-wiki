# What are the jurisdiction participation pathways?

## Short Answer

Jurisdictions can participate through local data review, agency submissions, correction reports, field validation, stewardship coordination, planning use, and feedback on releases. The appropriate pathway depends on the data and the intended decision.

## Significance

Local participation improves the chance that regional data reflect current jurisdictional knowledge and that unresolved limitations are visible to users.

## What This Means

Prepare the area, source, version, feature or issue, evidence, licensing information, and responsible contact. Confirm the current workflow before sending data or requesting a release change.

## What This Does Not Mean

Participation does not make a jurisdiction the owner of all statewide data, guarantee that a submission is incorporated, or replace local authority and review requirements.

## How To Use This

Choose a bounded role, record decisions and unresolved conflicts, coordinate across boundaries, and distinguish local operational data from a shared released dataset.

## Example

A city contributes a reviewed local inventory and documents its coverage and license. The steward evaluates compatibility before deciding whether it can inform a future release.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent submission requirements or promise publication. Cite current stewardship guidance, ask which dataset and role are involved, and abstain when the pathway is not documented.
