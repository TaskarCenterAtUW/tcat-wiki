# How could transit and pedestrian systems integrate more deeply?

## Short Answer

Transit and pedestrian systems could integrate more deeply through shared identifiers, coordinated release metadata, compatible geometry, station and stop pathway data, current construction information, and documented workflows for validating transfers.

## Significance

An integrated view can help users understand access from a pedestrian network to transit service and through station or facility pathways.

## What This Means

Keep pedestrian, transit-service, and station-internal layers distinct; document source, version, time period, licensing, and ownership; validate joins and transfer conditions with agencies and local evidence.

## What This Does Not Mean

Integration does not make sources complete, current, interchangeable, or automatically synchronized. A connected model does not guarantee a usable or accessible transfer.

## How To Use This

Use the current feeds and pedestrian releases, define the transfer question, preserve provenance, and test important results locally.

## Example

An analysis joins a pedestrian route to a station entrance with the agency's current service and pathway information, then records which conditions remain unknown.

## Assistant Guidance

Describe deeper integration as a possibility unless a current workflow documents it. Cite each source and abstain when identifiers, semantics, or update behavior are unclear.
