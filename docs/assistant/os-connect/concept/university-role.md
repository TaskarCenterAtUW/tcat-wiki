# What role could universities play?

## Short Answer

Universities could contribute research, student and community partnerships, field validation, technical development, training, campus data, and evaluation of pedestrian accessibility workflows.

## Significance

Universities can connect research and local practice while helping test methods across varied environments and users.

## What This Means

Define the research or service goal, campus and community roles, data rights, privacy, licensing, validation, funding, and how results will be maintained or released.

## What This Does Not Mean

University participation does not make a dataset authoritative, guarantee a product outcome, or replace jurisdictional, facility, legal, or community responsibilities.

## How To Use This

Use documented agreements, preserve provenance, provide accessible participation, validate findings, and communicate whether work is experimental, local, or released.

## Example

A university partners with a city and disability community to test pedestrian data, documents field validation, and shares methods without presenting the study as a statewide operational release.

## Assistant Guidance

Present this as a possible role unless a current partnership is documented. Do not invent university commitments or ownership.
