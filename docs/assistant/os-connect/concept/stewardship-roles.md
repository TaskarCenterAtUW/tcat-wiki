# What operational roles are needed for stewardship?

## Short Answer

Operational stewardship may require roles for source ownership, local validation, issue triage, technical maintenance, schema governance, quality review, release management, licensing and privacy, community engagement, and user support.

## Significance

Separating roles makes accountability and handoffs visible and reduces the risk that corrections or limitations are lost between collection and release.

## What This Means

Assign each role and decision authority, define evidence and escalation, track changes and versions, communicate limitations, and review whether staffing and funding remain adequate.

## What This Does Not Mean

Role labels do not identify a specific organization automatically, and one person or team may fill several roles. Stewardship does not guarantee complete or current data.

## How To Use This

Use current governance records to identify actual owners and stewards, document gaps, and use local authoritative records for current operational decisions.

## Example

A program assigns local validation to jurisdictions, schema and release management to a technical steward, and issue engagement to a support or community team.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent organizational responsibilities. Cite governance evidence and abstain when the role assignment or current steward is unknown.
