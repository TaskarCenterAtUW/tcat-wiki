# Who reviews submitted issue reports?

## Short Answer

Issue reports are reviewed through the applicable OS-CONNECT data or product stewardship process. The responsible reviewer or team can depend on the issue, dataset, jurisdiction, and workflow; do not assume a named person without current documentation.

## Significance

Knowing the review boundary helps reporters send a useful request and avoids promising that a report has reached the person who can change a dataset.

## What This Means

- Identify the product, dataset, location, and type of issue.
- Use the documented reporting channel and include evidence and version information.
- Ask the current steward who owns follow-up when the report crosses teams or systems.

## What This Does Not Mean

Submission does not identify a reviewer automatically, guarantee a response, or guarantee a correction. A reviewer may request more evidence or route the report elsewhere.

## How To Use This

Keep the report reference and provide precise context when following up. Do not publish an assumed contact name or claim ownership without a current source.

## Example

An agency reports an incorrect curb-ramp attribute and includes the dataset version. The issue is routed through the current stewardship process, and the agency asks for the responsible follow-up owner if clarification is needed.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent names, teams, or response times. Cite the current reporting guidance, ask for the dataset and issue context, and abstain when reviewer ownership cannot be verified.
