# What problem is OS-CONNECT trying to solve?

## Short Answer

OS-CONNECT addresses fragmented pedestrian-network data that can be difficult to connect, compare, validate, maintain, and use for accessibility-aware planning and analysis.

## Significance

When pedestrian data are isolated in different formats, jurisdictions, or inventories, users may miss connections and have difficulty understanding how conditions affect travel.

## What This Means

OS-CONNECT provides a connected, structured view of pedestrian features and attributes that can support routing, quality review, planning, and stewardship when used with its documented scope and limitations.

## What This Does Not Mean

OS-CONNECT does not solve every coverage, accessibility, governance, or maintenance problem and does not replace local authoritative records, field work, or legal review.

## How To Use This

Use the problem statement to understand purpose, then check the exact release, source, geography, methods, and intended use for a current question.

## Example

An agency uses a connected pedestrian release to identify a boundary gap that separate local inventories had not revealed, then validates the location with the responsible jurisdictions.

## Assistant Guidance

Avoid attributing undocumented goals or outcomes. Cite current project materials and distinguish the problem OS-CONNECT addresses from claims about results.
