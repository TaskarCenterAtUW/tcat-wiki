# Who owns OS-CONNECT data?

## Short Answer

Ownership of OS-CONNECT data and its component sources depends on the dataset's governance, contributors, licenses, and release terms. Do not infer ownership solely from who publishes a viewer or download.

## Significance

Ownership affects authority, permissions, correction responsibilities, attribution, and the handling of local or contributed records.

## What This Means

Check the release metadata, publisher, source agreements, license, jurisdictional records, and responsibilities for updates or corrections. Keep source ownership distinct from stewardship or hosting.

## What This Does Not Mean

Public access does not prove that one organization owns every feature or may change every source. Ownership does not guarantee accuracy or authority for every use.

## How To Use This

Cite the source and governance statement for the exact release, obtain permission where required, and route corrections to the responsible steward or local authority.

## Example

A jurisdiction contributes a local inventory under documented terms while a shared service publishes a release. Users preserve both the source attribution and the release metadata.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not name an owner without evidence. Distinguish publisher, steward, contributor, and local authority, and abstain when the governance record is unavailable.
