# How should transit agencies contribute?

## Short Answer

Transit agencies can contribute current stop, station, entrance, pathway, service, construction, and accessibility information and can help validate pedestrian access around their facilities.

## Significance

Agency information adds operational and facility context that a general pedestrian dataset may not contain.

## What This Means

Define the agency, facilities, feeds, identifiers, dates, licensing, update responsibilities, and intended use. Coordinate pedestrian data with agency-owned transit and station information without merging meanings automatically.

## What This Does Not Mean

Contribution does not guarantee that a source is incorporated, that station access is complete, or that pedestrian data can replace GTFS or GTFS Pathways.

## How To Use This

Use current agency records for operational decisions, preserve source and release metadata, validate joins and transfers, and route corrections through the documented workflow.

## Example

An agency supplies current station entrances and construction information so a pedestrian-access analysis can distinguish surrounding sidewalk access from station-internal pathways.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not promise a contribution route or publication date. Ask for the source and use, cite current guidance, and abstain when ownership or feed details are unknown.
