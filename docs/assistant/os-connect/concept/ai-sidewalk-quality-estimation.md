# Can AI estimate sidewalk quality?

## Short Answer

AI may estimate possible sidewalk characteristics from imagery or other data, but an estimate is not the same as a field-measured condition. Whether a current workflow provides such an estimate and how accurate it is must be verified.

## Significance

Automated estimates may help identify locations for inspection when direct measurements are incomplete. They may also miss surface defects, misread width or obstructions, or become outdated as conditions change.

## What This Means

Define the quality measure and source, retain the image or processing date, inspect results across different settings, and validate priority features with field or authoritative local evidence. Keep estimates labeled as estimates.

## What This Does Not Mean

An estimated quality value does not certify a sidewalk, establish compliance, or describe every traveler's experience. A high estimate does not prove that a path is usable, and a low estimate does not by itself prove that it is inaccessible.

## How To Use This

Use estimates for screening or research only when the limitations fit the decision. Document the model or method, confidence and errors when available, and the transition from candidate observation to verified record.

## Example

An image-based process marks a sidewalk as potentially uneven. A reviewer checks the image date and visits the location before recording a maintenance issue or accessibility attribute.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not present AI estimates as measured facts. Cite the source and processing context, require human review for consequential uses, and abstain when the measure or validation evidence is unknown.
