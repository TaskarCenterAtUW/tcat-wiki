# What are the jurisdiction engagement pathways?

## Short Answer

Jurisdiction engagement may include data review, correction reporting, local validation, stewardship coordination, planning discussions, and release feedback. The current pathway depends on the jurisdiction, dataset, and documented program process.

## Significance

Engagement gives jurisdictions a way to contribute local knowledge and clarify how statewide or shared data should be interpreted and maintained.

## What This Means

Identify the responsible agency, area, dataset, version, issue, evidence, and desired action. Use the current published contact or reporting route and retain records of the exchange.

## What This Does Not Mean

Engagement does not guarantee acceptance, publication, funding, or a particular response time. It does not transfer every data or maintenance responsibility to one organization.

## How To Use This

Define roles and decision points, document unresolved issues, validate boundary connections, and communicate whether feedback informs a correction, local workflow, or future planning.

## Example

A county reviews data with several cities, assigns local review for boundary mismatches, and sends a documented set of corrections through the current release process.

## Assistant Guidance

Name only verified pathways and current contacts. Distinguish engagement from formal approval, cite the applicable guidance, and abstain when ownership or process details are unknown.
