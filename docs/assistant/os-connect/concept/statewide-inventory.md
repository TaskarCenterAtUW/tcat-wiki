# Is this a statewide inventory?

## Short Answer

OS-CONNECT may provide statewide or broad-area pedestrian-network coverage, but whether a specific release is a statewide inventory must be checked in its current scope and metadata.

## Significance

The distinction matters because users may assume that a statewide label means complete, uniform, current, and authoritative coverage.

## What This Means

Check geographic scope, feature types, release, source coverage, exclusions, missing values, and validation. Treat areas outside the documented scope or with incomplete coverage separately.

## What This Does Not Mean

Statewide does not mean every feature or accessibility condition is mapped, validated, current, or legally sufficient.

## How To Use This

Use the exact release metadata, disclose gaps, validate high-consequence locations, and avoid comparing jurisdictions without accounting for source and coverage differences.

## Example

A release covers much of a state but has different collection dates and feature completeness by area. A planner reports those limits rather than describing it as a uniform inventory.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for the release and area, cite the scope, and abstain from a statewide-coverage claim when current metadata are unavailable.
