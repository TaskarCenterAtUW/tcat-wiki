# How can people report inaccessible areas?

## Short Answer

Report an inaccessible area through the current documented channel with a precise location, date, observed barrier or condition, affected feature, dataset or release, and supporting evidence. Use emergency or hazard channels instead when there is an immediate safety need.

## Significance

Specific reports help distinguish a physical observation, temporary condition, and data error and give stewards or agencies information for review.

## What This Means

Describe what was observed and how it affected travel without assuming the cause. Include coordinates or an identifiable place, relevant version, evidence, and contact or follow-up information only when appropriate.

## What This Does Not Mean

Reporting does not guarantee an immediate response, correction, repair, or release update. A report is not a complete accessibility assessment or legal finding.

## How To Use This

Use accessible and privacy-conscious reporting methods, keep a copy, avoid submitting sensitive personal details, and follow up through the verified support or issue channel.

## Example

A person reports a blocked sidewalk with its location and date, explains that the current map still shows a connection, and distinguishes the immediate physical issue from a possible dataset correction.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not promise who will act or when. Cite the current reporting path, distinguish hazards from data issues, and direct urgent safety concerns to the appropriate local emergency or hazard service.
