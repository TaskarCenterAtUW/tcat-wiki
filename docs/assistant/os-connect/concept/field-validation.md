# How should field validation be incorporated?

## Short Answer

Field validation should be incorporated according to the intended use, data uncertainty, and consequence of error. Prioritize important or ambiguous locations, document what was checked, and keep field observations distinct from the released dataset until reviewed.

## Significance

Validation improves confidence in selected features and can reveal gaps that are not visible in imagery or automated data. It is one part of a broader quality and stewardship process.

## What This Means

Define the sample or target locations, protocol, date, reviewer, attributes, evidence, and acceptance criteria. Compare observations with the dataset version and record matches, discrepancies, and unresolved issues.

## What This Does Not Mean

Field work does not make the entire dataset accurate, current, accessible, or compliant. A sample should not be generalized beyond its scope.

## How To Use This

Use risk-based sampling, include different environments and complex locations, protect participant privacy, and send confirmed corrections through the current documented workflow.

## Example

A planner validates a set of high-priority crossings and records the observed curb-ramp connections and conditions before using the results to support a planning discussion.

## Assistant Guidance

State the validation scope and date, cite the protocol and release, and abstain from broader conclusions when the sample or evidence is limited.
