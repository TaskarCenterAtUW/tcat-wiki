# How does OS-CONNECT support mobility justice?

## Short Answer

OS-CONNECT can support mobility justice by making pedestrian conditions, access gaps, and community-identified barriers more visible for discussion, validation, and action by responsible organizations. The dataset is a tool for accountability, not justice by itself.

## Significance

People who walk, roll, or depend on transit can face barriers that are hidden by car-focused data or incomplete inventories. Shared pedestrian information can support more informed participation and investment questions.

## What This Means

Combine the data with lived experience, community priorities, local authority, field evidence, and relevant demographic or transit information. Document who defines the problem, who decides, and what action is possible.

## What This Does Not Mean

OS-CONNECT does not represent every experience, determine whose needs have priority, or guarantee repair, funding, or equitable outcomes.

## How To Use This

Use accessible participation methods, protect privacy, validate important findings, explain uncertainty, and report how community knowledge affected review or decisions.

## Example

A disability community identifies a recurring crossing barrier that is not visible in the dataset. The agency verifies it and documents whether the result informs a correction, design review, or maintenance action.

## Assistant Guidance

Avoid claiming that data alone produce justice or speak for a community. Cite sources and participation, distinguish evidence from decision, and abstain when local context is missing.
