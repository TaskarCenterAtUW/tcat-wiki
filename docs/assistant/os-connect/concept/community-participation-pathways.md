# What are the community participation pathways?

## Short Answer

Community participation can include mapping, issue reporting, field observations, accessibility advocacy, workshops, Office Hours, public meetings, and review of priorities or interpretations. The current pathway depends on the activity and its responsible organization.

## Significance

Participation allows local knowledge and lived experience to inform data review and planning. It also helps participants understand which contributions are observations, corrections, or policy input.

## What This Means

Start by identifying the product, location, question, access needs, and desired outcome. Use the documented channel, provide specific evidence, and ask how the contribution will be reviewed and retained.

## What This Does Not Mean

Participation does not guarantee a correction, project, response, or publication. It does not make a participant responsible for verifying the entire dataset or represent the views of every community member.

## How To Use This

Provide accessible and privacy-conscious ways to participate, explain decision boundaries, preserve attribution or confidentiality as appropriate, and communicate what is known about review and follow-up.

## Example

A community member submits a location-specific observation through the issue process, while a group uses Office Hours to discuss mapping practice and a public meeting to discuss investment priorities.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent current events, contacts, eligibility rules, or outcomes. Cite the relevant pathway, distinguish participation from formal agency authority, and abstain when the current route is not documented.
