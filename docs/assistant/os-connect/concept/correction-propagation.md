# Does a correction update OS-CONNECT or OpenStreetMap?

## Short Answer

An update to an OS-CONNECT dataset does not directly update OpenStreetMap (OSM).

## Significance

Clear lineage and propagation understanding prevents users from treating a report in one viewer as a requested synchronized edit everywhere.

## What This Means

OS-CONNECT and OpenStreetMap are separate datasets.

## What This Does Not Mean

A correction made in OS-CONNECT does not make any edits directly to OpenStreetMap.

## How To Use This

Understand that an edit to OS-CONNECT does not directly propagate to OSM.

## Example

An agency staffer verifies that a correction to OS-CONNECT is present in the next versioned release, and understands that this change is not directly passed to OpenStreetMap.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not infer synchronization across platforms without an explicit documented workflow.
