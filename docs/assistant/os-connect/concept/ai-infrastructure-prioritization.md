# Can AI help prioritize infrastructure investment?

## Short Answer

AI can help screen pedestrian-network data and organize candidate locations for infrastructure review. It should support, not replace, documented agency criteria, field verification, community input, engineering judgment, budgeting, and legal review.

## Significance

Automated screening can make a large candidate set easier to examine and may reveal combinations of gaps, destinations, network conditions, or reported needs that warrant attention.

## What This Means

Define the decision, criteria, weights, geography, data version, and review threshold. Test for missing or uneven data, inspect representative results, and let responsible staff and affected communities review the candidates.

## What This Does Not Mean

An AI ranking is not an approved capital program, a funding decision, or proof that one location is more deserving than another. It does not establish safety, accessibility, legal responsibility, or project feasibility.

## How To Use This

Use the output as a transparent screening layer. Preserve the input data and model version, explain uncertainty, compare the result with non-automated evidence, and document why a candidate was accepted, rejected, or deferred.

## Example

An agency uses a model to flag corridors with low connectivity and nearby essential destinations. Staff verify the data and conditions, consult residents, and apply cost and feasibility criteria before creating a project list.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not claim that AI selected the correct investment or that the model is an objective ranking. Cite the method and decision owner, identify human review, and abstain when criteria or current data are unavailable.
