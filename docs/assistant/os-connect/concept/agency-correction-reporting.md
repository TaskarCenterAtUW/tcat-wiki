# How should agencies report corrections?

## Short Answer

Agencies should report corrections through the current documented OS-CONNECT or TDEI channel with a precise location, affected feature or attribute, dataset or release, proposed correction, source evidence, and contact for clarification.

## Significance

Specific reports are easier to reproduce, compare with source data, assign for review, and track across releases. They also reduce the risk of treating a local observation as an unverified global change.

## What This Means

Include the geometry or identifier, jurisdiction, dataset version, observed and expected values, collection date, evidence, scope, and urgency. State whether the report concerns OS-CONNECT, OpenStreetMap, TDEI, or a downstream product.

## What This Does Not Mean

A report does not automatically update a dataset, change OpenStreetMap, or guarantee a release date. Agencies remain responsible for their own authoritative records and decisions.

## How To Use This

Use the verified reporting route, send one report per clear issue when practical, group related corrections only when the workflow supports it, and retain the submission and version information for follow-up.

## Example

An agency reports several missing curb-ramp features with coordinates, field dates, photographs, and the release in which the omissions were found. Reviewers can then compare the evidence and track the outcome.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent an upload route or promise acceptance. Ask for the location, release, evidence, and intended dataset, cite current reporting guidance, and direct unresolved operational questions to the verified support email.
