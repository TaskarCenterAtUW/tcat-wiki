# Can OS-CONNECT support FIFA or major-event accessibility planning?

## Short Answer

OS-CONNECT can provide pedestrian-network data and accessibility context for major-event planning. Use it to understand modeled access and identify places for verification; it does not by itself plan event operations or guarantee safe access.

## Significance

Large events concentrate travel demand and can expose gaps in pedestrian connectivity. A versioned network view can support coordination between planners, accessibility advocates, and event partners.

## What This Means

- Identify event destinations, travel corridors, and relevant accessibility concerns.
- Review the applicable dataset, profiles, and known data limitations.
- Combine modeled results with field checks, operations planning, and community input.

## What This Does Not Mean

OS-CONNECT is not an event safety plan, emergency-management system, or accessibility compliance determination. A mapped route is not a guarantee of current conditions during an event.

## How To Use This

Use the data to prioritize questions and field verification around venues, stations, crossings, and pedestrian approaches. Record the dataset version and assumptions used in planning materials.

## Example

Before a major event, planners compare modeled pedestrian access to a venue from nearby transit stops and then verify construction, crowd-control, and crossing conditions in the field.

## Assistant Guidance

Present results as modeled planning inputs, cite the dataset and version, and do not promise safety or accessibility outcomes. Abstain when the event location, dataset, or operational conditions are missing.
