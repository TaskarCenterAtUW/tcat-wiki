# How can an agency confirm that a submitted correction was incorporated?

## Short Answer

Confirm incorporation by checking the later dataset release, its version, the feature location, and relevant attributes. Do not rely only on the original issue submission.

## Significance

Versioned comparison can provide evidence about what was published. The [viewer feedback manual](../../../os-connect/data-viewer/user-manual/feedback.md) documents submission but not issue-status tracking.

## What This Means

Keep the issue location and description, then check a later relevant release and its metadata for evidence of the change. The viewer manual does not document an issue-status or review workflow.

## What This Does Not Mean

A submitted report or acknowledgement of receipt by email does not prove that the correction was incorporated.

## How To Use This

Document the compared release and any remaining discrepancy.

## Example

An agency staffer finds the reported feature in the next versioned release with the corrected attribute.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not claim change incorporation without release evidence or an authoritative, case-specific status update if one is available.
