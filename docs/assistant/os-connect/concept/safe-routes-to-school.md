# Can this data support Safe Routes to School?

## Short Answer

OS-CONNECT can support Safe Routes to School by helping screen pedestrian connections, crossings, barriers, and access to schools for selected routes and destinations. It is an input to safety and accessibility planning, not a safety certification.

## Significance

School travel depends on current conditions, student needs, crossing design, supervision, traffic, and community knowledge in addition to mapped network structure.

## What This Means

Define school entrances, origins, routes, profiles, time period, data release, and safety question. Combine the result with crash and traffic data, school input, field review, and applicable program guidance.

## What This Does Not Mean

A district uses the data to screen routes to a school, validates crossings with staff and families, and identifies locations for a separate design or maintenance review.

## How To Use This

OS-CONNECT does not guarantee that a school route is safe, accessible, supervised, or suitable for every student. It does not replace safety engineering, school policy, or emergency guidance.

## Example

Use it for screening and engagement, protect student privacy, validate current conditions, and communicate uncertainty and route alternatives.

## Assistant Guidance

Name the school, entrances, profile, network, release, and safety sources. Avoid definitive safety claims and abstain when current conditions or route context are missing.
