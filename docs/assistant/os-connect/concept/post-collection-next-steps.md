# What comes after statewide collection?

## Short Answer

After statewide collection, the work typically shifts toward validation, correction, maintenance, local stewardship, versioned releases, documentation, use-case testing, community participation, and sustainable governance.

## Significance

Collection creates a baseline; continued value depends on keeping data interpretable, current enough for its uses, and connected to decisions and local knowledge.

## What This Means

Prioritize quality review, coverage and gap analysis, jurisdiction handoffs, corrections, schema and tool maintenance, release communication, and evaluation of how the data are used.

## What This Does Not Mean

Statewide collection does not mean the data are complete, current, authoritative for every use, or finished as a stewardship program.

## How To Use This

Define the next actions from the intended use and risk, assign responsibilities, fund recurring work, and document future work as plans unless it is confirmed in a current roadmap.

## Example

A program moves from initial collection to local validation, issue tracking, coordinated updates, and a release process that communicates known limitations.

## Assistant Guidance

Distinguish current operations from possible future capabilities. Cite current program evidence and abstain from promising a roadmap, cadence, or outcome without verification.
