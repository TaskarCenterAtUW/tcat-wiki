# How can older adults contribute?

## Short Answer

Older adults can contribute local knowledge, travel observations, accessibility priorities, and corrections through documented community, field, mapping, or planning channels.

## Significance

Older adults may notice conditions involving crossings, surfaces, lighting, rest opportunities, wayfinding, construction, or confidence in travel that structured data do not capture fully.

## What This Means

Provide accessible participation formats, record the location and context, protect privacy, distinguish observation from correction request, and explain how the input will be reviewed.

## What This Does Not Mean

An older adult's contribution does not represent every older traveler, guarantee a dataset change, or replace field, agency, or professional review.

## How To Use This

Ask participants what format and support they need, avoid unnecessary personal information, and communicate verified next steps without promising an outcome.

## Example

An older adult reports that a route's steep crossing and lack of seating make a destination difficult to reach. The observation helps focus local review while remaining distinct from modeled data.

## Assistant Guidance

Respect the contributor's experience without generalizing it. Cite the participation process and abstain when the current reporting or engagement path is unknown.
