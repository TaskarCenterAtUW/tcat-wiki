# How can residents participate?

## Short Answer

Residents can participate by sharing observations, reporting possible data errors, contributing to mapping or field validation, discussing priorities, and reviewing how pedestrian information relates to local experience through documented channels.

## Significance

Residents provide local knowledge about changing conditions, barriers, destinations, and travel needs that may not appear in structured data.

## What This Means

Identify the location, date, observed condition, dataset or release, evidence, and desired follow-up. Use accessible and privacy-conscious participation methods and ask how the report will be reviewed.

## What This Does Not Mean

Participation does not guarantee a correction, project, response, or consensus. One resident's report does not represent every resident or replace professional review.

## How To Use This

Use the current engagement or reporting pathway, submit only necessary personal information, preserve the release viewed, and distinguish temporary conditions from persistent data gaps.

## Example

A resident reports a blocked sidewalk near a bus stop with the date and location, then follows up through the documented issue process without assuming the map will update automatically.

## Assistant Guidance

Respect the observation without generalizing it. Cite the participation and review process, avoid promising outcomes, and abstain when the current channel is unknown.
