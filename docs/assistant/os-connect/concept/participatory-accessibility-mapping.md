# What is participatory accessibility mapping?

## Short Answer

Participatory accessibility mapping combines structured mapping with the lived experience and local knowledge of people who use or are affected by pedestrian infrastructure.

## Significance

Participation can reveal barriers, priorities, and conditions that imagery or automated data may not capture.

## What This Means

Define the location, purpose, participants, accessibility needs, data to collect, privacy practices, review process, and intended use. Keep observations distinct from verified records.

## What This Does Not Mean

Participatory mapping is not a complete survey, consensus statement, legal finding, or guarantee that every report changes a dataset or project.

## How To Use This

Use accessible methods, compensate or credit contributions as appropriate, protect privacy, validate important findings, and report how input was considered.

## Example

A group maps difficult crossings and compares the observations with OS-CONNECT, then submits specific data issues and planning priorities for review.

## Assistant Guidance

Respect participant experience without generalizing it. Cite the process and release, and abstain when the participation or review pathway is unknown.
