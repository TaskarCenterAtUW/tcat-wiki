# What role did community validation play?

## Short Answer

Community validation can add local knowledge and lived experience to the review of pedestrian data. It helps identify possible gaps, errors, and conditions that imagery or automated processing may not capture.

## Significance

Community review broadens the evidence available to data stewards and can make corrections and priorities more relevant to people who use the network.

## What This Means

Participants can review mapped locations, report observations, compare data with current conditions, and explain how a feature affects travel. Reviewers should record the source, date, location, and status of each contribution.

## What This Does Not Mean

Community validation is not a complete survey, legal certification, or automatic update to OS-CONNECT, OpenStreetMap, or TDEI. One report does not represent every person's experience.

## How To Use This

Use accessible, privacy-conscious methods, explain the review scope, separate observations from confirmed corrections, and communicate what will happen next when that information is verified.

## Example

A community member reports that a connected path is blocked by a current construction project. The observation is reviewed against the release and current local information before any correction or temporary note is considered.

## Assistant Guidance

Respect lived experience without treating it as automatically generalizable. Cite the participation and review process, avoid promising outcomes, and abstain when the current pathway is unknown.
