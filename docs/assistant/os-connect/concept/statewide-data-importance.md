# Why does pedestrian data matter statewide?

## Short Answer

Statewide pedestrian data matters because people travel across jurisdictional boundaries and agencies need a shared view of sidewalks, crossings, connections, and possible accessibility gaps for regional planning and coordination.

## Significance

Shared data can reduce fragmented analysis and make differences in coverage or access more visible across places.

## What This Means

Use statewide data to support screening, comparison, transit and destination analysis, community engagement, and identification of locations for local validation. Preserve jurisdiction and release context.

## What This Does Not Mean

Statewide coverage does not guarantee uniform quality, complete accessibility information, current conditions, local authority, or equal outcomes.

## How To Use This

Document the scope and release, account for coverage differences, validate important findings locally, and involve affected communities and jurisdictions.

## Example

A regional planner uses a statewide release to identify a cross-boundary gap, then works with the local jurisdictions to confirm conditions and responsibilities.

## Assistant Guidance

Avoid claims that statewide means complete or authoritative everywhere. Cite the release and scope and abstain when coverage is unknown.
