# What am I looking at in this viewer?

## Short Answer

The OS-CONNECT Data Viewer is a web map for exploring rendered OS-CONNECT pedestrian network data, dataset boundaries, feature attributes, QA/QC reports, and issue-reporting tools.

## Significance

The viewer makes a large jurisdictional dataset inspectable to planners, transportation professionals, jurisdictions, and community members.

## What This Means

Use the Raster or Imagery basemap, toggle Data and Boundaries, search for datasets, zoom to level 16 or higher to inspect feature attributes, open boundary details, and follow links to QA/QC reports or issue forms.

## What This Does Not Mean

The viewer is an inspection and communication tool, not a complete source-data editor or a guarantee that mapped conditions are current.

## How To Use This

Use the viewer to locate a dataset, inspect its metadata and features, and report observed problems. Cite the dataset version and report context.

## Example

A community member searches for a jurisdiction, opens a boundary popup, copies its TDEI dataset ID, and inspects a crossing at high zoom.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask which viewer version, dataset, zoom level, and layer are involved. Distinguish rendered display behavior from source-data semantics.
