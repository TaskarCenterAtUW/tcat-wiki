# How can this data support freedom of movement for nondrivers?

## Short Answer

OS-CONNECT can support freedom of movement for nondrivers by providing pedestrian-network information that helps people and organizations understand routes, connections, barriers, and access to important destinations.

## Significance

People who walk, roll, or depend on transit need usable pedestrian data to participate in daily life, services, work, and community activities. Shared information can make access gaps visible.

## What This Means

Use the data for route and reachability analysis, planning, issue reporting, and community discussion. Combine it with current transit, local, field, and lived-experience information.

## What This Does Not Mean

The data does not guarantee a usable route, replace live navigation or transit service, or ensure that every nondriver's needs are represented.

## How To Use This

State the release and limitations, validate important conditions locally, include affected communities, and distinguish a data improvement from a physical or operational improvement.

## Example

A planner uses pedestrian-network data to identify missing connections to a transit stop and works with local partners to verify the barrier and consider next steps.

## Assistant Guidance

Avoid claiming that the dataset creates mobility or represents all nondrivers. Cite the source, profile, and context, and abstain from current-condition claims without verification.
