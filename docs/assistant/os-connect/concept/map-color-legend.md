# What do the colors mean?

## Short Answer

The viewer manual lists nine legend categories: Sidewalk, Crossing (Marked), Crossing (Unmarked), Footway, Kerb (Lowered), Kerb (Flushed), Kerb (Raised), Traffic Island, and Others.

## Significance

Colors distinguish feature categories in the viewer, but users should check the written legend rather than rely on color alone.

## What This Means

The [viewer manual legend](../../../os-connect/data-viewer/user-manual/interface.md#legend) lists light blue for Sidewalk, yellow for Crossing (Marked), green for Crossing (Unmarked), dark green for Footway, pink for Kerb (Lowered), dark blue for Kerb (Flushed), purple for Kerb (Raised), dark purple for Traffic Island, and gray for Others.

## What This Does Not Mean

A color identifies the viewer category, not the quality, safety, accessibility, or legal status of a feature.

## How To Use This

Read the legend labels together with the selected feature's attributes and dataset metadata; do not identify a category from color alone.

## Example

A user selects a feature and checks its legend label and popup attributes rather than relying on the apparent color alone.

## Assistant Guidance

If colors differ from this description, treat the live viewer legend as authoritative and report the viewer version or screenshot.
