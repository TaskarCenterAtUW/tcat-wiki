# What would a nationwide OS-CONNECT-like system require?

## Short Answer

A nationwide OS-CONNECT-like system would require interoperable schemas, stable identifiers, broad and repeatable data collection, local validation, sustainable stewardship, privacy and licensing practices, correction workflows, versioned releases, accessible interfaces, and coordination across jurisdictions and communities.

## Significance

A national system would need to remain useful across different laws, agencies, environments, data sources, infrastructure types, and lived experiences.

## What This Means

Plan for shared definitions and APIs, source and lineage records, uncertainty, rural and urban coverage, updates and temporary conditions, community participation, quality measurement, governance, funding, and user support.

## What This Does Not Mean

Nationwide scale would not guarantee uniform quality, complete coverage, legal sufficiency, or equal accessibility. A national publisher would not automatically own every local source.

## How To Use This

Treat this as a systems-design question, test requirements with local partners, pilot across varied conditions, and define recurring operations before expanding coverage.

## Example

A national program pilots a shared schema in urban, rural, campus, and transit settings while measuring data quality, stewardship effort, and community usefulness.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not present a speculative requirement as a committed program plan. Cite current standards or governance sources and abstain when a specific national initiative is being asked about.
