# How are community comments reviewed?

## Short Answer

Community comments should be reviewed according to the current documented process, with attention to the location, observation, evidence, dataset version, and intended action. The exact reviewer and outcome depend on the channel and issue.

## Significance

Review separates a community observation from a confirmed correction and helps preserve a clear record of what was reported and what was verified.

## What This Means

Reviewers may classify the comment, request clarification, compare it with source data, check the location, record an outcome, and route accepted changes through the applicable workflow.

## What This Does Not Mean

Review does not guarantee acceptance, publication, a response time, or a change to OpenStreetMap, OS-CONNECT, TDEI, or another product. A comment is not automatically authoritative.

## How To Use This

Provide precise, respectful, and privacy-conscious information. Include the location, date, observed condition, source or release, evidence, and desired follow-up without overstating what the evidence proves.

## Example

A comment about a missing curb ramp includes a location and field observation. Reviewers compare it with the current release and document whether more evidence or a correction workflow is needed.

## Assistant Guidance

Explain the review status and next step when known. Do not identify a reviewer or outcome unless documented, and abstain when the current review process is unavailable.
