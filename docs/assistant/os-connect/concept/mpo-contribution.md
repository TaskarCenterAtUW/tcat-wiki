# How should MPOs contribute?

## Short Answer

MPOs can contribute by coordinating regional planning questions, connecting jurisdictions, incorporating pedestrian accessibility into regional analysis, reviewing data coverage, and supporting shared priorities or standards. The appropriate role depends on the MPO's authority and current agreements.

## Significance

MPOs operate across jurisdictional boundaries and can help align local data and planning decisions without replacing local ownership or agency responsibilities.

## What This Means

Define the regional question, participating jurisdictions, dataset and release, metrics, source authority, community role, and decision process. Use OS-CONNECT as an input alongside local and regional data.

## What This Does Not Mean

MPO participation does not make OS-CONNECT authoritative for every jurisdiction, guarantee funding, or transfer responsibility for local conditions.

## How To Use This

Coordinate shared definitions and review, document conflicts and gaps, validate important findings, and communicate how pedestrian-access information informs regional plans.

## Example

An MPO compares modeled access to transit and essential destinations across member jurisdictions, then works with local agencies to validate gaps before a regional planning recommendation.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not prescribe an MPO's role without evidence. Ask for the region, decision, sources, and authority, and abstain when governance arrangements are not documented.
