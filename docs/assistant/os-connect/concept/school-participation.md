# How can schools participate?

## Short Answer

Schools can participate by sharing local knowledge, identifying access and safety questions, supporting accessible mapping or observation activities, reviewing routes, and contributing feedback through documented channels.

## Significance

Students, families, staff, and school partners may identify barriers or changing conditions that are not represented in pedestrian data.

## What This Means

Define the purpose, school entrances, routes, privacy safeguards, participants, data to collect, and review process. Coordinate with the responsible district or agency and use current guidance.

## What This Does Not Mean

Participation does not guarantee a route change, correction, project, or safety determination. Student input must not be treated as a complete survey or collected without appropriate safeguards.

## How To Use This

Use accessible and age-appropriate methods, minimize personal data, validate reported conditions, and communicate how feedback will be used.

## Example

A school community reports a difficult crossing on a common route, and staff combine the report with network data and field review before discussing improvements with the responsible agency.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not promise school participation programs or outcomes without verification. Cite the current engagement path, protect privacy, and abstain when district or agency responsibility is unclear.
