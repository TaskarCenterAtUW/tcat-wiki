# How should QA/QC metrics and local priorities be combined?

## Short Answer

Use computed metrics alongside local knowledge, community input, project goals, and field verification.

## Significance

Metrics can reveal patterns, while local people identify needs missing from open data.

## What This Means

Treat prioritization as an evidence-integration process.

## What This Does Not Mean

A high score or hotspot automatically decides what a community should build.

## How To Use This

Validate candidate locations and document why local priorities change an interpretation. Local knowledge may reasonably outweigh an unexpected metric result.

## Example

A missing local park is added to the POI context before a connectivity priority is selected.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask whose priorities and which evidence are represented.
