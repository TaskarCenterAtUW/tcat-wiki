# Where do QA/QC report points of interest come from?

## Short Answer

POIs may be assembled from open sources such as OpenStreetMap and Overture data using relevant feature tags.

## Significance

POIs influence walkshed, density, and bottleneck analyses, so omissions can affect priorities.

## What This Means

The inventory is a starting set that can require local review and additions. Supporting POI data may be controlled internal data rather than a public download.

## What This Does Not Mean

An absent POI does not mean the place is unimportant to the community.

## How To Use This

Ask jurisdictions to identify missing destinations before relying on destination-based metrics.

## Example

A city adds a locally important park that was absent from the open-data-derived POI set.

## Assistant Guidance

Distinguish source coverage from local importance and preserve uncertainty.
