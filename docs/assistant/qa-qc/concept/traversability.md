# What does traversability mean in a QA/QC report?

## Short Answer

Traversability is an analytic score for how usable mapped crossing movements appear to be at an intersection.

## Significance

It helps identify locations where mapped crossing access may need review.

## What This Means

Higher values indicate more crossing movements appear available and usable under the report's rules.

## What This Does Not Mean

The score is not a direct inspection result or a legal compliance determination.

## How To Use This

Check the report's profile, inputs, and scale before comparing scores.

## Example

A cluster of low scores can guide field checks of crossings and missing crossing attributes.

## Assistant Guidance

Describe the score as modeled evidence and preserve its dataset and version context.
