# What are the boundaries of QA/QC quality scoring?

## Short Answer

Quality or safety scores require defined criteria, local context, and appropriate authority; they should not be improvised from incomplete standards.

## Significance

A score can appear authoritative even when its meaning is undefined.

## What This Means

Agree on the scale, inputs, and interpretation before applying a score.

## What This Does Not Mean

ADA requirements do not automatically supply a complete condition or safety score.

## How To Use This

Document the source, weights, thresholds, and limitations of any score.

## Example

A project defines a provisional 1-to-5 pedestrian-support scale before comparing scenarios.

## Assistant Guidance

Avoid legal conclusions and ask for the governing metric definition.
