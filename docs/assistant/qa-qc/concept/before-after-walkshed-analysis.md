# How can QA/QC reports compare before and after scenarios?

## Short Answer

A scenario can modify mapped sidewalks, crossings, or curb ramps and compare resulting reachability with the current network.

## Significance

This shows how a proposed connection could affect access to destinations or households.

## What This Means

Compare equivalent profiles, POIs, assumptions, and dataset versions.

## What This Does Not Mean

A scenario is a model, not a construction guarantee.

## How To Use This

Document every modeled change and validate assumptions with the responsible jurisdiction.

## Example

Adding two sidewalk segments and curb ramps increases the modeled walkshed around a service area.

## Assistant Guidance

Label hypothetical changes clearly and avoid promising outcomes.
