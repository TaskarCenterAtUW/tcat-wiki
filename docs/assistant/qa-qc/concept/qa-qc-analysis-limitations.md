# What are the limitations of QA/QC analysis?

## Short Answer

QA/QC reports describe mapped data and modeled results, not guaranteed current field conditions or ADA compliance.

## Significance

These boundaries prevent technical summaries from being treated as legal or field determinations.

## What This Means

Use imagery-based results to guide review, prioritization, and field verification.

## What This Does Not Mean

A report is not an ADA transition plan or regulatory compliance finding.

## How To Use This

Check imagery date, dataset version, POI scope, metric definition, and field evidence.

## Example

A hotspot is inspected on the ground before a jurisdiction prioritizes an improvement.

## Assistant Guidance

State the report's data and model limitations explicitly.
