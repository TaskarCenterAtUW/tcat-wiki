# What curb metrics appear in QA/QC reports?

## Short Answer

Reports can distinguish all curbs, curbs on crosswalks, lowered curbs, and flush curbs, including crosswalk-specific counts.

## Significance

These categories separate general inventory from crossing-relevant conditions.

## What This Means

Read each count with its feature scope and definition.

## What This Does Not Mean

Counts do not by themselves establish curb usability or legal compliance.

## How To Use This

Compare the appropriate curb category with the analysis question.

## Example

A report may show lowered curbs overall and lowered curbs specifically on crosswalks.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask which curb denominator the user is interpreting.
