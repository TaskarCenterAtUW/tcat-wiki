# What does a z-score mean?

## Short Answer

A z-score shows how far a value is above or below the average for the report's comparison set.

## Significance

It helps identify locations that stand out relative to other locations in the same analysis.

## What This Means

Positive values are above average; negative values are below average.

## What This Does Not Mean

A z-score is not an absolute condition rating or a cross-report guarantee.

## How To Use This

Confirm the comparison and metric before interpreting the score.

## Example

A high bottleneck z-score identifies a tile that stands out from the report's other tiles.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not compare z-scores from different datasets.
