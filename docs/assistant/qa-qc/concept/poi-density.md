# What does POI density mean?

## Short Answer

POI density is the number or relative concentration of mapped points of interest within an analysis area.

## Significance

Clusters show where network improvements could potentially affect access to more destinations.

## What This Means

Reports may group areas into density categories such as quartiles for visual comparison.

## What This Does Not Mean

Density is not a complete measure of demand, need, or project priority.

## How To Use This

Check which destinations were included and combine density with connectivity and local knowledge.

## Example

An area with many mapped schools and clinics may appear in a higher density category.

## Assistant Guidance

Treat POI density as context for review, not as a decision rule.
