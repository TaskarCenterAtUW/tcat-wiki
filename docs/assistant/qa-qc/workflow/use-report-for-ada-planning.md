# How can a QA/QC report support ADA planning?

## Short Answer

Use the report as an inventory and screening input, then combine it with legal requirements, local priorities, field verification, and agency capacity.

## Significance

It can organize evidence about network gaps, attributes, destinations, and candidate improvements.

## What This Means

Review completeness, islands, walksheds, connectivity, and hotspots for the relevant dataset.

## What This Does Not Mean

The report is not itself an ADA transition plan or legal compliance determination.

## How To Use This

Confirm the dataset, add local POIs, validate hotspots, define agency criteria, and document priorities.

## Example

A city uses a report to identify a hospital connection for field review and a future sidewalk project.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for jurisdictional requirements and do not provide legal conclusions.
