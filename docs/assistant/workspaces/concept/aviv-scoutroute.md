# What is Aviv ScoutRoute?

## Short Answer

AVIV ScoutRoute is a mobile volunteerism app, formerly called GoInfoGame, based on StreetComplete and used to collect pedestrian and accessibility information in configured workspaces. Current app features, quests, and workspace behavior are version- and configuration-dependent.

## Significance

The app provides a mobile pathway for structured field observations and contributions that can complement desktop editing and review.

## What This Means

A workspace must be configured for external-app access and may provide quest definitions or imagery settings. Contributors follow the current quest and app instructions, then upload observations for workspace review.

## What This Does Not Mean

AVIV ScoutRoute contributions are not automatically verified, published to OpenStreetMap, or exported to TDEI. A quest response does not by itself establish accessibility or compliance.

## How To Use This

Check workspace publication settings, quest definition, app version, permissions, and connectivity before collecting data. Record the source and review contributions before export.

## Example

A manager publishes a workspace for external apps, configures a quest definition, and asks volunteers to collect observations that are later reviewed in Workspaces.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not claim current app behavior beyond the documented version. Distinguish the app from Workspaces and TDEI, cite current manuals, and abstain when configuration details are unavailable.
