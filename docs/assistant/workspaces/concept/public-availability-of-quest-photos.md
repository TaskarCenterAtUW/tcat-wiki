# Who can access photos collected through quests?

## Short Answer

Photos collected through quests may become publicly available beyond the contributor team, depending on the configured workflow.

## Significance

Contributors need to understand the visibility implications before taking photographs.

## What This Means

Get appropriate permission and avoid collecting sensitive or unnecessary imagery.

## What This Does Not Mean

A project team should not assume that quest photos remain private.

## How To Use This

Confirm the current publication, licensing, and privacy rules before enabling photo questions.

## Example

A quest asks for a photo of new infrastructure, and the project explains that the resulting image may be public.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not provide legal clearance; direct privacy questions to the responsible project authority.
