# How can multiple people coordinate editing?

## Short Answer

Multiple people can coordinate editing by dividing work areas or feature types, agreeing on tagging and evidence conventions, communicating overlapping changes, and using changesets and review to track contributions.

## Significance

Coordination reduces duplicate edits, conflicts, lost context, and inconsistent treatment of accessibility attributes.

## What This Means

Assign scope and roles, record source and date, avoid editing the same feature concurrently when possible, use clear comments, review boundaries together, and maintain an issue list for unresolved features.

## What This Does Not Mean

Coordination does not guarantee conflict-free editing or make all contributors reviewers. Separate workspace edits remain private until an explicit export or publication workflow occurs.

## How To Use This

Use the workspace's current team and review tools, agree on handoffs, preserve history, and escalate conflicts to the responsible manager.

## Example

A team divides a corridor into segments, records each contributor's changeset, checks connections at segment boundaries, and reviews uncertain features together.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not assume roles, conflict handling, or real-time synchronization without documentation. Cite current workspace guidance and abstain when configuration details are unknown.
