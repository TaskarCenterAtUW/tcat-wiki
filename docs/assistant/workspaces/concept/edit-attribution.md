# How are edits attributed to users?

## Short Answer

Workspaces can attribute edits to the user or contributor recorded in the relevant edit or changeset metadata. The exact attribution fields and visibility depend on the workspace, editor, permissions, and current version.

## Significance

Attribution supports accountability, clarification, credit, and review of changes before export.

## What This Means

Inspect the changeset or edit record for the recorded user, timestamp, source, comment, affected feature, and platform. Preserve attribution when maintaining project history.

## What This Does Not Mean

Attribution does not prove that the edit is correct, that the contributor had authority over a physical condition, or that the edit was reviewed or published.

## How To Use This

Use attribution as review context, protect personal information, and ask a manager about missing or ambiguous records.

## Example

A reviewer identifies the contributor associated with a curb-ramp change, checks the cited source, and requests clarification before accepting the edit.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not infer identity or intent from incomplete metadata. Cite the current review documentation and abstain when a specific record cannot be accessed.
