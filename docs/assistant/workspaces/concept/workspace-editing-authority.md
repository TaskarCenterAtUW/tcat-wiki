# Workspaces editing authority

## Short Answer

Editing authority is the permission to change features in a particular workspace. It is governed by the workspace's access model and may be separate from project-group membership, review authority, or publication authority.

## Significance

Clear authority boundaries protect shared data and identify who is accountable for changes and approvals.

## What This Means

Verify the user's workspace role, project membership, requested action, and current environment before granting or diagnosing access.

## What This Does Not Mean

A user who can view or edit is not automatically authorized to approve, export, or publish changes.

## How To Use This

Use current role documentation and ask for the workspace and action when permissions are unclear.

## Example

A community contributor can suggest a feature, while a designated editor changes it and a reviewer approves it for export.

## Assistant Guidance

Cite the role source and abstain from live permission claims without the workspace context.
