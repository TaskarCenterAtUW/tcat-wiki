# What roles exist in Workspaces?

## Short Answer

Workspaces roles define what a person can do in a workspace, such as view, edit, review, or manage it. Broader project-group roles may govern related access but are not interchangeable with workspace roles.

## Significance

Explicit role boundaries help teams delegate edits and review without confusing organizational membership with dataset authority.

## What This Means

Check the workspace role and project-group role for the specific action. Assign the least authority needed for the task and verify current product documentation.

## What This Does Not Mean

A role label alone does not establish that a user can view, edit, review, export, or publish every workspace.

## How To Use This

Check the workspace role and project-group role for the specific action. Assign the least authority needed for the task and verify current product documentation.

## Example

A project member can see a workspace but needs a workspace-specific editor or reviewer role to perform the requested action.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask which workspace, project group, and action are involved. Cite role documentation for access or publication decisions.
