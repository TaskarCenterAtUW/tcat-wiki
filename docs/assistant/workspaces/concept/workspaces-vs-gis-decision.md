# When should a city use Workspaces instead of GIS software?

## Short Answer

Use Workspaces when the primary need is collaborative editing and review of a controlled pedestrian or accessibility dataset. Use GIS software when advanced spatial analysis, cartography, transformation, or agency-specific workflows are required; many teams may use both.

## Significance

The choice affects training, governance, collaboration, analytical depth, and the path from edits to publication.

## What This Means

Compare the task, staff skills, data source, required analysis, permissions, and publication process before choosing a tool.

## What This Does Not Mean

Workspaces is not a complete GIS replacement, and GIS software does not automatically provide Workspaces' roles, review gates, or dataset workflow.

## How To Use This

Ask what the city needs to do, who will edit or review, and where the resulting data must go.

## Example

A city uses Workspaces for community-supported feature editing and a GIS application for network analysis and map production.

## Assistant Guidance

Cite current product capabilities and avoid a universal recommendation without task and governance context.
