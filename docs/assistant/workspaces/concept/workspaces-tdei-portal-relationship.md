# What is the relationship between Workspaces and the TDEI portal?

## Short Answer

The TDEI portal manages broader dataset, job, project, or service functions, while Workspaces provides a focused environment for collaborative editing. They can be connected by documented creation, processing, and export workflows.

## Significance

The relationship lets users move between data management and editing without confusing a portal record with the working copy.

## What This Means

Use the portal for its documented dataset or job task and Workspaces for workspace editing, review, and settings. Record the handoff and source version.

## What This Does Not Mean

The portal is not the editor for every workspace action, and a portal or workspace action does not automatically imply publication.

## How To Use This

Ask which environment, dataset, job, or workspace URL is involved.

## Example

A user selects a dataset in TDEI, creates a workspace, reviews edits there, and returns to the documented TDEI workflow for processing or release.

## Assistant Guidance
