# What is the review interface?

## Short Answer

The review interface is the Workspaces area used to inspect edits and their context before acceptance, export, or publication. Its available actions depend on the workspace and user's permissions.

## Significance

Review creates a checkpoint between editing and release. It helps teams identify questionable changes, missing evidence, and unresolved conflicts.

## What This Means

Inspect changes, compare geometry and attributes with source evidence, record decisions, and escalate issues that require domain or jurisdiction review.

## What This Does Not Mean

The interface does not certify accessibility, guarantee that every error is detected, or grant approval authority to all users.

## How To Use This

Use the current workspace review guidance and verify the reviewer role and dataset release before approving changes.

## Example

A reviewer examines a curb-ramp edit, compares it with available evidence, requests clarification, and leaves it out of export until resolved.

## Assistant Guidance

Cite current review documentation. Ask which workspace and role are involved when the user asks whether an action is available.
