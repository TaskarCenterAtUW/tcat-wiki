# How do managers interact with Workspaces?

## Short Answer

Managers coordinate workspace access, editing scope, review, team activity, data quality, and export or publication decisions according to the workspace configuration.

## Significance

A manager role provides coordination and accountability between contributors and a possible released dataset.

## What This Means

Managers may configure teams and apps, assign or confirm reviewers, inspect changesets, resolve questions, preserve lineage, and decide when review criteria are met.

## What This Does Not Mean

Manager access does not make every edit correct or make the manager the owner of every source or physical condition.

## How To Use This

Use current role and workspace documentation, record decisions and unresolved issues, and separate review from final agency or legal authority.

## Example

A manager reviews volunteer edits, requests clarification on an uncertain crossing, and authorizes export only after the project's criteria are satisfied.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not infer permissions from the title alone. Ask for the workspace and environment, cite current documentation, and abstain when role behavior is unknown.
