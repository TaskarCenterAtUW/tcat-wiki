# How do I review AVIV ScoutRoute contributions in Workspaces?

## Short Answer

Open the workspace review view, inspect the per-feature changeset, compare previous and current attributes, and resolve or comment on the contribution as appropriate.

## Significance

Review connects field collection with trusted dataset publication.

## What This Means

Use metadata, platform, author, timestamps, comments, and changed values to assess the contribution.

## What This Does Not Mean

A changeset record does not prove that the observation is correct.

## How To Use This

Review additions, modifications, notes, and deletions separately.

## Example

A reviewer confirms a field-collected surface value before exporting the workspace.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for workspace, changeset, and review status when troubleshooting.
