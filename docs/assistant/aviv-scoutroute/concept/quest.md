# What is a quest in AVIV ScoutRoute?

## Short Answer

An AVIV ScoutRoute quest is a small, feature-specific data collection task. A map icon identifies a target such as a bus stop, sidewalk, crossing, intersection, curb, or driveway point and opens configured questions.

## Significance

Quests divide field collection into focused observations that contributors can complete consistently.

## What This Means

The app displays available quests in a workspace. A contributor opens one, observes the target feature, answers its configured inputs, reviews the response, and submits it.

## What This Does Not Mean

Quest availability depends on the project, workspace coverage, and quest definition. A quest answer does not by itself publish or replace the whole dataset.

## How To Use This

Use the quest instructions and observe the feature directly. Zoom in when icons are stacked, and use Unknown or skip when the observation cannot be determined.

## Example

A sidewalk quest asks a contributor to record the surface and width of the highlighted sidewalk segment.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask which workspace and quest type are involved. Do not infer that a missing icon means the feature is absent.
