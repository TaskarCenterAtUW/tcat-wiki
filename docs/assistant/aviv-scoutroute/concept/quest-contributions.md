# What do AVIV ScoutRoute quest contributions change?

## Short Answer

AVIV ScoutRoute quests collect targeted answers or attribute updates for selected features. They support focused field contributions rather than unrestricted editing of an entire dataset.

## Significance

This division makes mobile data collection practical for specific observations. The resulting contributions can be reviewed and combined with the workspace data.

## What This Means

A contributor answers the configured questions for a feature, such as a curb ramp or bus stop. On submission, the answer is saved to the workspace and the completed quest disappears from the map. The contribution becomes part of the project's review and publication workflow.

## What This Does Not Mean

A quest answer is not automatically a final public edit or a replacement for the whole dataset. Review, export, and release remain separate steps.

## How To Use This

Answer only the questions supported by the observed feature and project instructions. Review the response before submitting, and report uncertainty through the available options, notes, or feedback process.

## Example

A contributor surveys a bus stop and records its sidewalk material through quests without editing unrelated features in the surrounding dataset.

## Assistant Guidance

Explain the scope of the assigned feature and the next review step. Do not promise immediate publication of a quest answer.
