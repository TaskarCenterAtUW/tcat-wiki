# How do I answer a quest in AVIV ScoutRoute?

## Short Answer

Open a quest at the observed feature, answer each configured question, review the response, and select **Submit**. Use direct observation and do not guess when the condition is unclear.

## Significance

Accurate field observations improve the quality of the project data collected through AVIV ScoutRoute.

## What This Means

Quest inputs may use single choice, numeric entry, multiple choice, or free-form text. Follow the requested units and character limits, and use Unknown or skip when available and appropriate.

## What This Does Not Mean

A submitted answer is not a guarantee of correctness, an unrestricted edit, or immediate publication.

## How To Use This

Zoom in to reveal stacked icons, open a quest, inspect the highlighted feature, answer it, review the response, and submit. Undo a recent submission if necessary.

## Example

A contributor observes a curb ramp, selects the applicable accessibility options, checks the measurement unit, and submits the answer.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for the platform, workspace, quest type, and exact input when troubleshooting. Do not tell users to guess an answer.
