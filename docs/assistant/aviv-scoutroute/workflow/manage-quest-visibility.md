# How do I manage AVIV ScoutRoute quest visibility?

## Short Answer

Zoom in to reveal stacked quest icons, enable the quest types you need in settings, and use the hide or restore controls to manage local quest visibility.

## Significance

Visibility controls help contributors focus on a survey while avoiding the mistaken assumption that hidden or stacked quests do not exist.

## What This Means

Use **Quest selection and display order** to enable or disable quest types and reorder them. Open a quest and choose **Hide** or **Hide this** to remove it locally. Use **Restore hidden quests**, **Unhide All**, or the relevant Undo control to restore hidden items according to the current platform UI.

## What This Does Not Mean

These controls do not change project configuration or remove tasks for other users. Labels and menu locations can vary by app version.

## How To Use This

First select the correct workspace and map area, then zoom in. Review enabled quest types before concluding that no suitable quest is available.

## Example

A contributor enables only sidewalk quests for a mapping session, zooms in to reveal stacked icons, and restores a hidden quest when it becomes relevant.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for the platform, app version, workspace, zoom level, and current settings. Avoid treating local visibility as evidence that the workspace lacks data.
