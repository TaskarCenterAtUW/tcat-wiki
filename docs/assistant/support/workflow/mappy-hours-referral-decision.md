# When should someone be referred to Mappy Hours versus receiving direct support?

## Short Answer

Refer someone to Mappy Hours or Office Hours when the question benefits from collaborative mapping discussion, shared context, or hands-on exploration. Answer a focused question directly when it is documented; otherwise direct the person to the verified support email or Office Hours.

## Significance

Choosing the right channel reduces delays and avoids sending a simple support request to a group session or treating a collaborative mapping problem as a one-line answer.

## What This Means

- Classify the question by product, task, and urgency.
- Answer directly when the answer is documented and specific.
- Direct the person to the verified support email for an unresolved operational issue, or suggest Mappy Hours / Office Hours when discussion, demonstration, or mapping collaboration is useful.

## What This Does Not Mean

- Mappy Hours is not an emergency-response channel or a guarantee of an immediate answer.
- A direct answer does not replace product review when the question requires internal verification.

## How To Use This

Summarize the user's goal, product, location or dataset, and desired outcome before selecting a channel. Provide the current Mappy Hours information only after verifying it.

## Example

A partner asks how to interpret one documented field, so staff answer directly. A second request needs collaborative mapping discussion, so staff recommend Mappy Hours or Office Hours and explain what context to bring. An unresolved operational issue is directed to the verified support email.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask what outcome the person needs. Cite the relevant product documentation, use the verified support email or current Office Hours information as appropriate, do not promise meeting availability or response times, and abstain when channel details are unknown.
