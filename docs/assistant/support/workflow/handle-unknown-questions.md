# What should staff do when they do not know the answer to a partner's question?

## Short Answer

When staff do not know the answer, they should state what is known, identify the missing context, avoid guessing, and route the question to the appropriate current source or verified support channel. They should preserve the partner's question and provide the support email or Office Hours path when appropriate.

## Significance

Honest uncertainty protects partners from acting on invented product, data, legal, or operational claims while keeping the conversation useful.

## What This Means

- Classify the product, dataset, version, location, and requested outcome.
- Search current documentation and identify the unresolved point.
- Ask the responsible team or steward for verification, or use the verified support email or Office Hours path when that is the documented next step.
- Record the answer and update the partner without overstating certainty.

## What This Does Not Mean

Saying "I do not know" is not a substitute for a next step, and escalation does not guarantee acceptance, correction, or a deadline.

## How To Use This

Use a concise acknowledgement, explain what needs checking, and give only a confirmed timeframe or verified support-email or Office-Hours path.

## Example

A partner asks about a current portal permission that is not documented. Staff explain the known access model, flag the live behavior for verification, and route the question without guessing.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent an answer, owner, or response time. Cite the available documentation, label the gap, and abstain until the missing fact is verified.
