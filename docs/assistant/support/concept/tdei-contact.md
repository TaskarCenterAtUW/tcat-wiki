# Who should external partners contact for TDEI support?

## Short Answer

Use the current TDEI Portal documentation for documented questions about accounts, project groups, datasets, jobs, releases, and downloads. Use the verified TDEI support email for unresolved operational issues; use Office Hours only when the question is appropriate for collaborative mapping discussion. The correct path depends on the specific workflow and access context.

## Significance

Clear routing helps partners distinguish portal support from OS-CONNECT data stewardship, product support, and local agency responsibilities.

## What This Means

- Identify the portal action, dataset, project group, account, or release involved.
- Consult the current TDEI manual and support information.
- Include the relevant identifier, version, error, and steps when requesting help.

## What This Does Not Mean

The TDEI support path does not guarantee access, correction, publication, or a response time. Do not infer a private contact from a general article.

## How To Use This

Start with the current portal manual and use the verified TDEI support email for unresolved account or portal issues. Separate those problems from OS-CONNECT data questions, which may belong in the OS-CONNECT reporting or support path.

## Example

A partner cannot find a released dataset. Staff ask for the project group and dataset context, check the current portal guidance, and route the unresolved access question through the documented TDEI path.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent contact names, addresses, permissions, or timelines. Cite current TDEI documentation and abstain when the portal state or responsible owner cannot be verified.
