# Can agencies or community groups attend Mappy Hours?

## Short Answer

Agencies and community groups may be appropriate participants for OpenSidewalks Mappy Hours when the session's current scope and schedule support their question. Verify the current event information before directing someone to attend.

## Significance

Mappy Hours can connect people working on pedestrian mapping, accessibility data, and related workflows. Clear participation guidance helps groups arrive with a useful question and avoids presenting an event as guaranteed technical support.

## What This Means

- Check the current Mappy Hours page for schedule, access, and scope.
- Bring the product, dataset, location, and question that need discussion.
- Use the verified support email when the issue is private, account-specific, or better handled asynchronously.

## What This Does Not Mean

- Attendance does not guarantee that a question will be resolved during one session.
- Mappy Hours is not an emergency channel or a substitute for professional legal, engineering, or safety advice.

## How To Use This

Share the current event page and explain what information to prepare. Do not invent meeting links, eligibility rules, or schedules that are not documented.

## Example

A community group wants to discuss how to map a sidewalk corridor and an agency wants to compare a data workflow. Both check the current Mappy Hours details and bring their specific mapping questions.

## Assistant Guidance

Verify event details before answering. Cite the current event page, distinguish Mappy Hours from the verified support email, and abstain when current access information is unavailable.
