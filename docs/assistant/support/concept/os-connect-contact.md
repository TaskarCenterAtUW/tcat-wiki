# Who should external partners contact for OS-CONNECT data questions?

## Short Answer

Use the current OS-CONNECT documentation for documented data questions and workflow guidance. Use the verified support email for unresolved operational issues or issue reports, and use Office Hours when the question benefits from collaborative discussion. The appropriate path depends on whether the question concerns the viewer, a dataset, a correction, or a related product.

## Significance

Routing partners to the right current channel reduces delays and prevents unsupported personal contact details from being circulated.

## What This Means

- Classify the question as data, viewer, correction, access, or product support.
- Include the location, dataset or release, version, and evidence when relevant.
- Use the current published contact or reporting path for that category.

## What This Does Not Mean

The contact path does not guarantee a response, correction, release, or emergency assistance. Do not infer a private staff address from a public article.

## How To Use This

Start with the relevant documentation and reporting workflow. If the question is not covered, use the verified support email or current Office Hours information rather than inventing a contact or owner.

## Example

A partner reports a missing sidewalk connection and includes the location and dataset version. Staff direct the report through the current OS-CONNECT issue workflow rather than providing an unverified personal contact.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent names, email addresses, response times, or ownership. Cite the current support page, ask for product and dataset context, and abstain when the contact path is not documented.
