# Who should external partners contact for OpenSidewalks mapping questions?

## Short Answer

Use the current OpenSidewalks documentation or mapping guidance for documented questions. Use the verified support email for unresolved operational issues, and use current Mappy Hours / Office Hours information when the question benefits from collaborative mapping discussion. The correct path depends on whether the question concerns schema, editing, community coordination, or a specific dataset.

## Significance

Clear routing helps mappers find current technical or community guidance without confusing OpenSidewalks standards with OS-CONNECT or TDEI support.

## What This Means

- Identify the schema, editor, project, or community question.
- Consult the current documented mapping or event channel.
- Include the data version, location, and evidence when asking about a specific problem.

## What This Does Not Mean

Contacting a mapping channel does not guarantee a correction, partnership, or release. Do not publish private or unverified staff contact details.

## How To Use This

Start with the relevant OpenSidewalks documentation and current community guidance, then request clarification through the documented channel.

## Example

A mapper needs advice about a sidewalk tagging convention, so they consult the current mapping guidance and bring the unresolved example to the appropriate community discussion.

## Assistant Guidance

Do not invent contact names or schedules. Cite current OpenSidewalks resources, ask what workflow is involved, and abstain when the correct owner cannot be verified.
