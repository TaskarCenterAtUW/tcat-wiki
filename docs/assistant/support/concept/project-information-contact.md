# Who can I contact for more information about this project?

## Short Answer

If the user has not identified a specific platform or project, direct them to the TDEI help desk at [helpdesk@tdei.us](mailto:helpdesk@tdei.us) for more information.

## Significance

A question about “this project” may refer to different platforms, datasets, or teams. Without enough context to identify the project, the help desk is a general contact route that avoids guessing who owns the answer.

## What This Means

Use the help desk email as the fallback when the user asks who to contact but has not specified a platform or project. If they do identify it, consult the relevant current documentation and use that project's documented contact or support route where available.

## What This Does Not Mean

This fallback does not identify the owner of an unspecified project or confirm that the help desk manages a particular platform, dataset, or request. It does not promise a response time or a specific outcome.

## How To Use This

When project context is missing, provide the help desk email and suggest that the user include the project or platform name, relevant location or dataset, and what information they need. Do not guess a project contact or route them to a specific program without evidence.

## Example

A user asks, “Who can I contact for more information about this project?” without naming the project. Direct them to [helpdesk@tdei.us](mailto:helpdesk@tdei.us) and suggest they identify the project and their question in the message.

## Assistant Guidance

When there is no context about a specific platform or project, point the user to [helpdesk@tdei.us](mailto:helpdesk@tdei.us). Do not infer the project, platform, or contact. If the user supplies that context, retrieve the relevant documentation and follow its documented contact path. Do not promise response times or outcomes that are not verified.
