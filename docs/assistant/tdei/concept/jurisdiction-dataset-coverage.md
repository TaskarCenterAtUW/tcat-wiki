# How do TDEI jurisdiction datasets differ in coverage?

## Short Answer

City, urban growth area, county, and statewide datasets represent different geographic scopes and may be released at different times.

## Significance

Choosing the wrong scope can exclude needed connections or include unrelated areas.

## What This Means

Select the dataset based on purpose, geography, version, and update date.

## What This Does Not Mean

A county dataset is not automatically the latest or a superset of every current city release.

## How To Use This

Check the dataset name, boundary, version, and portal update metadata.

## Example

A construction project crossing counties may merge current county releases rather than use an older statewide release.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask for the intended geography and purpose before recommending a dataset.
