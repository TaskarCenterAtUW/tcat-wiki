# How is TDEI dataset feedback managed?

## Short Answer

Project-group points of contact can review feedback submitted against datasets in their group. The feedback view can summarize open, overdue, and completed work and filter by dataset.

## Significance

A managed feedback queue connects user reports with dataset stewardship. It provides a place to track follow-up rather than losing comments in separate channels.

## What This Means

Review the report, investigate the relevant data, and communicate or apply a correction through the appropriate workflow. The dashboard is an operational aid.

## What This Does Not Mean

Submitting feedback does not automatically correct the dataset or guarantee a response time. Permissions and review decisions remain with the responsible team.

## How To Use This

Use the dataset filter and status information to prioritize work. Local feedback may identify missing or incorrect features. Record the correction or explanation after review.

## Example

A point of contact filters feedback for one dataset, investigates a reported curb-ramp issue, and follows up after the dataset is updated.

## Assistant Guidance

Ask whether the user is a project-group point of contact. Do not promise that feedback will be resolved by a specific date.
